# DreamCarz Founders Round — Investor Deck Logic & Risk Audit

**Review basis:** *Dream_Carz_Founders_Round_Investor_Presentation (1)*, retrieved from the user-supplied Adobe Acrobat link and reviewed on August 13, 2026. The source deck describes a $250,000 preliminary “hybrid” founders round with 80% debt and 20% equity, a $5 million management working valuation, and a $25 million target milestone. This is a business and presentation review, not legal advice, securities advice, or a valuation opinion.

## Executive assessment

The source deck has a coherent high-level idea: DreamCarz is evolving from an automotive operator into a membership-led mobility ecosystem. Its central weakness is that it asks investors to bridge a large gap between a **current operating business**, a **multi-product future vision**, and a **hybrid debt/equity investment proposition** without supplying the evidence, capital structure, or legal framing needed to connect them. The redesign should therefore lead with the operating wedge and financing purpose, show only supportable facts, isolate the future roadmap, and move any conditional financing mechanics into a clearly labeled illustrative appendix.

| Severity | Item | What the source deck says | Risk / inconsistency | Correction for the revised deck |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Hybrid security is undefined | “Debt income potential + early equity participation”; 80% debt / 20% equity. | The instrument, legal entity, investor rights, repayment obligation, seniority, collateral, conversion/transfer terms, and default mechanics are not defined. | Replace promotional language with “Proposed founders-round structure — subject to definitive documentation.” Use a term-summary page with **TBD / counsel-confirmed** fields rather than implied terms. |
| Critical | Debt-service capacity is not evidenced | 36 months, 12% simple annual interest, quarterly interest. | The deck does not identify the payment source, operating cash flow, reserve, seniority, collateral, or default treatment. | Add a financing-readiness page with **cash-flow support, vehicle financing obligations, insurance, reserves, repayment source, and downside case** — or omit debt terms until they are documented. |
| Critical | Valuation is unsupported | $5M current working valuation; $25M target milestone. | No capitalization table, valuation methodology, financial statements, KPI evidence, or comparables are supplied. A target valuation is not an investment thesis. | Present this as a **management reference value** only in the appendix, pending financials, cap-table, and method. Replace the headline value trajectory with operational milestones. |
| High | Ownership math uses pre-money percentages without a clear convention | $50K of equity is shown as 1.00%; $10K equity as 0.20%. | At a $5.0M **pre-money** valuation and $50K of new equity, the fully subscribed equity tranche implies a $5.05M post-money equity value. The equity tranche is **0.990099% post-money**; a $10K equity purchase is **0.198019% post-money**, before any other dilution. | State “illustrative percentage based on a $5.0M pre-money reference valuation” and use post-money ownership, or remove ownership examples until the actual capitalization table and security are set. |
| High | Interest example is incomplete | $40K note at 12% simple annual interest generates $4,800/year. | Arithmetic is correct, but it does not show the full contractual burden. On the stated assumptions, quarterly interest is **$1,200**, 36-month simple interest totals **$14,400**, and total principal plus simple interest is **$54,400**. This assumes every interest payment is made when due and ignores fees, prepayment, taxes, default, collateral, and compounding/PIK alternatives. | Do not use an investor-return example as a headline. If retained in an appendix, display the payment schedule, assumptions, and loss-of-principal/no-payment-guarantee disclosures. |
| High | Value-appreciation example risks misleading framing | 0.20% equity is illustrated at $5M through $100M company value. | Even with a disclaimer, it foregrounds speculative appreciation, assumes no dilution, and does not address liquidity, preferred rights, tax, follow-on financing, or exit uncertainty. | Replace with a concise ownership/dilution explainer and a risk page. Do not display nominal “future value” outcomes. |
| High | Use of proceeds is too high-level for mixed capital | 40% fleet growth; 25% technology/app; 20% marketing/members; 15% working capital. | Totals correctly, but investors cannot assess vehicle financing, insurance, registration, maintenance, loss reserves, tech scope, marketing payback, or runway. | Break out line items, capital dependencies, timing, accountable owner, measurable output, and contingency. Separate debt-funded assets from equity-funded growth spend. |
| High | Multiple business models lack sequencing | Memberships, rentals, lease-to-own, vehicle sales, loyalty, host, agents, app, future branches. | The deck can read as unfocused; several paths may have different licensing, credit, insurance, consumer-protection, and working-capital requirements. | Show **one operating wedge**, **two near-term engines**, and a gated roadmap. Label non-live elements as planned and tie expansion to measurable readiness criteria. |
| Medium | KPI proof is missing | “What drove the first 5×” cites broad operational expansion and strategy. | No dated operating metrics substantiate the $1M→$5M management working valuation narrative. | Add only verified KPIs: vehicle/fleet count, utilization, revenue, gross margin, membership count, repeat rate/churn, credit performance if applicable, maintenance/loss performance, and operating cash flow. Mark unavailable metrics as “to be verified,” not estimated. |
| Medium | App and platform language may overstate readiness | The source deck implies a technology-enabled ecosystem and plans to complete/deploy the app. | Investor readers need a clean live-versus-planned separation. | Use a “Built today / next build / gated expansion” page. Retain verified website/member-app functionality and mark integrations, live partner data, maps, Rekognition, notifications, and other dependent services as roadmap items unless fully operating. |
| Medium | Investor suitability and process are incomplete | $25K minimum appears with a general disclaimer. | Minimum amount alone is not a compliant process or investor-eligibility standard. | Add a counsel-reviewed process slide: discussion materials only; no solicitation; participation only through definitive documents and appropriate eligibility, suitability, and jurisdictional review. |

## Arithmetic check

The following calculations use only the deck’s stated assumptions.

| Calculation | Formula | Result |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fully subscribed equity tranche ownership at a $5.0M pre-money reference valuation | $50,000 ÷ ($5,000,000 + $50,000) | **0.990099% post-money** |
| $10K equity component ownership at same reference valuation | $10,000 ÷ ($5,000,000 + $50,000) | **0.198019% post-money** |
| Annual simple interest on $40K note at 12% | $40,000 × 12% | **$4,800** |
| Quarterly interest payment, if paid quarterly | $4,800 ÷ 4 | **$1,200** |
| 36-month simple interest | $40,000 × 12% × 3 | **$14,400** |
| Principal plus 36-month simple interest | $40,000 + $14,400 | **$54,400** |

> The calculations do **not** establish a return, security value, repayment capacity, legal enforceability, or investment suitability. They merely test the internal arithmetic of the source presentation’s illustrative terms.

## Revised investor-deck rules

The redesign will retain the source deck’s substantive narrative only where it is defensible. It will use verified statements about the business and product, conditionally framed future plans, non-promotional financing language, and a clearly separated diligence/terms appendix. Claims of income, valuation growth, investor return, partner-network scale, payment capacity, operational readiness, or security features will be removed unless documented and approved.

| Original emphasis | Revised emphasis |
|---|---|
| “Income potential today. Equity participation for tomorrow.” | “A proposed founders-round financing to support defined operating and product milestones, subject to definitive documentation.” |
| $5M → $25M value-creation plan | “Milestone plan: operating proof, member adoption, product delivery, and disciplined expansion.” |
| Future equity value illustrations | “Ownership mechanics, dilution considerations, illiquidity, and risk factors.” |
| Broad ecosystem list | “Current operating wedge, current product capability, near-term focus, and gated expansion roadmap.” |
| Top-down use of proceeds | “Capital deployment with line-item purpose, outputs, responsible owner, dependency, and measurement.” |

## Required evidence before external investor circulation

| Evidence set | Minimum review item |
|---|---|
| Corporate and securities | Legal entity identity, formation/cap-table records, board/manager approvals, counsel-reviewed security documents, exemption/jurisdictional analysis, investor eligibility/process. |
| Debt readiness | Promissory-note terms, debt seniority, collateral/security, repayment source, cash-interest schedule, covenants, prepayment/default rights, disclosure of risk of loss. |
| Operating proof | Historical revenue, gross margin, utilization, fleet/vehicle data, maintenance/loss and insurance detail, membership/retention data, operating expenses, cash flow, and working-capital schedule. |
| Valuation support | Current cap table, reference valuation basis, financial statements, method/comparable rationale, and dilution scenario. |
| Product and roadmap | Live-versus-planned feature list, third-party dependencies, roadmap budget, delivery owners, and milestone gates. |
| Use of proceeds | Asset-level vehicle funding plan, insurance/maintenance/reserve assumptions, technology scope, marketing spend logic, working-capital runway, and contingency. |
