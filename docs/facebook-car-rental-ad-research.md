# DreamCarz Facebook Ad Research Notes

## Research-led creative direction

The strongest first test is a **mobile-first vertical creative** with one clear offer, visible DreamCarz branding, a concise benefit-focused message, and a direct call to action. Meta recommends engaging creative with a relevant call to action and keeping lead forms simple; it also recommends vertical or square creative and concise text for mobile placements.[1][2]

The proposed DreamCarz first-frame message is: **Rent. Rent to Own. No Credit Check.** It avoids language that implies a viewer’s credit standing or other personal attributes. The destination remains `https://www.dreamcarz.io/get-started`, where prospects provide only name, phone, email, and contact consent before entering Concierge.

## Policy boundary

Meta prohibits ads that assert or imply an audience member’s vulnerable financial status. The ad therefore describes the offer and does not use copy such as “Bad credit?” or “Denied elsewhere?”.[3]

If the actual Rent to Own offer involves long-term financing, installment credit, or another financial-service offer, DreamCarz should select Meta’s **Financial products and services** Special Ad Category for US delivery before launch. Meta states this category has been required for US financial-products-and-services campaigns since January 21, 2025.[4]
