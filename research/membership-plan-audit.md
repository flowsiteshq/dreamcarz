# Membership Plan Audit — Supplied Experience Deck

## Scope

This audit covers live membership-plan references in the public pricing, membership, fleet, and vehicle-detail experiences; the protected member package experience; and related public FAQ, terms, calculator, and payment surfaces discovered by source search.

## Findings and Corrections

| Surface | Finding | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Public pricing | It exposes the deck’s seven plan names but presents its explicitly illustrative enrollment and monthly figures as live terms. | Retain the seven approved plan names and value framing; replace financial amounts with “terms confirmed during membership review.” |
| Fleet and vehicle detail | Both duplicate the same illustrative membership amounts in selected-plan context. | Preserve the selected-plan name and explain vehicle-specific terms remain separate; remove duplicated financial figures. |
| Member package page | It uses only Entry, Mid-Range, and Elite categories rather than showing the deck’s seven named membership paths. | Show the seven named paths as program guidance, with current confirmed-vehicle exploration and no claim that a plan guarantees access. |
| FAQ and calculator | They reference a different, older four-tier plan structure and unsupported $39.95/$69.95/$99.95/$149.95 figures. | Replace stale amounts and tier claims with plan-review guidance. |
| Terms and billing history | They display fixed fee terms and sample invoices that cannot be reconciled to an approved membership pricing schedule. | Do not represent the deck’s illustrative pricing as contractual terms or customer billing. Retain counsel-approved agreement text and real provider data requirements as an external blocker. |

## Approved Live Content

The live experience may present the user-supplied plan names—**Freedom, Plus, Pro, Elite, Silver, Gold, and Black**—along with the deck’s non-financial focus labels: rentals and DCP; subscribe-and-save exploration; broader vehicle path; lease-to-own exploration; Silver-and-below guidance; host and fleet participation guidance; and application-led Black/exotic exploration. Each must retain the existing qualification, approval, current-inventory, and non-guarantee disclosures.

## Financial and Legal Boundary

The supplied deck explicitly labels membership enrollment amounts, recurring amounts, daily vehicle amounts, and savings as illustrative examples. They must not be published as current contractual terms, live vehicle rates, DCP rates, or member savings until DreamCarz provides an approved pricing matrix, tax/deposit/fee rules, CoCard SKU mapping, and counsel-approved agreement wording.
