## Source evidence and limitations

The BWI search displayed a Toyota Corolla or similar at $187 total, a Toyota Camry or similar at $190 total, a Mazda CX-50 or similar at $197 total, and a full-size SUV Chevrolet Tahoe, Jeep Wagoneer, Ford Expedition or similar at $274 total.[^bwi] The DCA search displayed a Toyota Corolla or similar at $187 total, a Toyota Camry or similar at $190 total, and a Mazda CX-50 or similar at $194 total.[^dca]

Rates are live marketplace results, not guarantees. They can change by pickup branch, exact pickup time, booking lead time, inventory, driver eligibility, optional protection, cancellation selection, and signed-in discounts. These daily equivalents do **not** establish DreamCarz profitability, taxes, delivery fees, mileage limits, deposits, maintenance recovery, insurance costs, or final customer pricing. Confirm a live quote and complete internal pricing approval before publishing or charging any rental rate.
