## Redesign visual verification

The redesigned cover and illustrative debt-mechanics slides were visually reviewed. Both use the established DreamCarz **white/off-white, black, and gold** system with premium white automotive imagery, large readable editorial typography, restrained gold rules, and a consistent DC monogram treatment. The debt slide clearly labels the 36-month, 12% simple-interest, quarterly-payment example as **illustrative arithmetic rather than a return forecast**, and includes debt-documentation requirements with the correct page number.

The ownership slide was visually reviewed and correctly displays the **0.198019%** and **0.990099%** illustrative post-money percentages alongside the pre-money reference and cap-table caveats. The closing slide was also visually reviewed; it replaces promotional return language with a milestone, documentation, and diligence sequence while retaining the DreamCarz white/black/gold automotive design.

The business narrative and operating-wedge slides were visually reviewed. They cleanly separate current automotive operating activity from platform direction, place **Vehicle Access** and **Membership** at the near-term center, and position DCP, service support, host, associate/agent, and technology as gated extensions rather than simultaneous operating claims.

The capital-need and valuation-context slides were visually reviewed. The raise is tied to measurable operating and product milestones rather than a speculative valuation outcome, while the $5M and $25M figures are visually and verbally framed as management references—not independent appraisal, guaranteed valuation, liquidity, or investor-return claims.

The use-of-proceeds and founders-round structure slides were visually reviewed. The source allocation totals are retained and connected to accountable outputs, while the debt/equity split is clearly labeled as a preliminary discussion structure that requires entity confirmation, definitive security terms, repayment and collateral review, and securities/tax/eligibility documentation.

The milestones and investor-readiness slides were visually reviewed. The revised narrative now asks valuation to follow evidence, with six concrete operating gates and a clear external-circulation checklist covering financials, vehicle economics, debt-service support, cap-table detail, definitive documents, legal-entity records, investor eligibility, and risk disclosures.
