# Vehicle Access Packages QA

The Membership page now presents three vehicle-access packages rather than generic Freedom, Plus, Pro, and Elite membership pricing. The packages are **Entry Vehicle Access**, **Mid-Range Vehicle Access**, and **Elite Vehicle Access**. Each card explains the intended vehicle category, tells the member to explore confirmed inventory, and directs them to a rental-or-purchase request rather than promising a vehicle, rate, fee, approval, or outcome.

The Entry package links to four entry-category vehicles; Mid-Range links to three mid-range vehicles; and Elite links to the single highest category represented in the current confirmed inventory. The linked Fleet views were directly rendered and verified with their corresponding filters active. The package page and all three filtered inventory views maintain the established white, black, and gold DreamCarz design system.

Automated content coverage verifies the package labels, the three package-to-inventory destinations, the filtered inventory logic, and removal of the former fixed membership prices. The full suite completed with 54 passing tests and TypeScript validation passed.
