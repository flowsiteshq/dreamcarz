# Homepage Reference QA

## Direct visual comparison

The final homepage was directly inspected against the supplied reference. It now follows the same light luxury structure: a compact white-and-gold header, a split hero with text on the left and the white Tesla visual on the right, a four-point confidence strip, and an immediately visible prompt bar beneath the hero. The prompt bar accepts a question and routes vehicle, membership, pathway, and account-intent queries to the relevant DreamCarz destination.

Below the prompt bar, the page uses the same deliberate narrative hierarchy as the reference: three pathway columns, a four-step transportation journey, a confirmed inventory lineup, help and support content, a closing call to action, and the existing footer. The rebuilt inventory contains only the eight user-confirmed vehicles and each card leads to the existing full-vehicle, rental, and purchase experience.

## Copy compliance review

The refreshed copy was tightened to avoid a fixed rate, reward amount, vehicle price, payout, availability promise, or unsupported income claim. Vehicle inventory is described as current and confirmed, while rental and purchase terms remain subject to a request and review. DreamCarz Value is framed as subject to program rules, and Associate/Fleet Partner language retains the process-and-agreement framing used in the approved ecosystem updates.
